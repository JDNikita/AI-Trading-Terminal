# Event-Driven Macro & Product Launch Playbooks

## 1. Product Launches & Corporate Announcements ("Buy the Rumor, Sell the News")
- **Phase 1: Pre-Announcement Accumulation (T-21 to T-7 Days):** Retail speculation and early algorithmic accumulation build steady upward momentum accompanied by tightening volatility bands.
- **Phase 2: Announcement Day Exhaustion (T-0):** A massive volume gap-up or explosive wide-bodied candle. **Critical Rule:** If public expectations are merely met (not beaten by a wide margin), smart money instantly distributes shares to incoming retail FOMO, resulting in an aggressive long upper wick or a complete intraday reversal.
- **Phase 3: The Post-Release Retracement:** Historically, assets subject to "Buy the Rumor, Sell the News" retrace between 50% and 61.8% of the initial announcement pump within 3 to 5 trading sessions before true fundamental price discovery begins.

## 2. Macro Surprises (CPI, FOMC Rate Decs, Non-Farm Payrolls)
- **Liquidity Voids:** High-impact macroeconomic surprises instantly hollow out order books. Standard limit orders fail, resulting in severe slippage.
- **The 3-Day Institutional Rule:** Single-day panic spikes or FOMO dashes on macro news are frequently mean-reverting. True institutional trend continuation requires a minimum of 3 consecutive closing sessions in the direction of the macro break.
