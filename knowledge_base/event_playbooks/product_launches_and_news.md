# Real-Life Event & Product Release Price Mechanics

## Product Launch / Major Announcement ("Buy the Rumor, Sell the News")
1. **Pre-Announcement Phase (1-3 Weeks Prior):** Gradual accumulation, rising volume, and tightening price volatility as speculation builds among retail and early institutional participants.
2. **Announcement Day (Exhaustion Phase):** Massive volume gap-up or explosive candle. If expectations are merely met (not beaten), smart money immediately takes profits, leading to a swift intraday reversal or long upper wick.
3. **Post-Release Retracement:** Price typically consolidates or retraces 50-60% of the initial announcement pump before establishing true fundamental support based on actual adoption metrics.

## Earnings & Macro Surprises (CPI, Rate Cuts)
- **Structural Break Mechanics:** High-impact surprises create liquidity voids, causing wide-bodied breakout candles that shatter 50-day and 200-day Simple Moving Average lines instantly.
- **Institutional Follow-Through Rule:** True institutional moves sustain direction for 3 to 5 trading days; single-day spikes on low volume are treated as fakeouts and mean-revert.

## Memecoin Sentiment Velocity
- **Exponential Reaction:** Attention spikes drive parabolic price expansion as fear-of-missing-out (FOMO) forces retail buying pressure into thin order books.
- **Liquidity Cascade Risks:** When social volume plateaus, bids vanish instantly, causing severe liquidity cascades and rapid downward spirals.
