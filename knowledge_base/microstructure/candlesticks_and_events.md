# Master Candlestick & Price Action Guide

## Reversal Patterns
- **Bullish Engulfing:** Occurs at major support. Large green candle completely engulfs previous red candle body. Volume must be above 20-period average.
- **Shooting Star:** Long upper wick (at least 2.5x body size) at key resistance. Represents an intraday bull trap.

## Event Impact Rules
- **Product Launches ("Buy Rumor, Sell News"):** Buying volume surges 1-3 weeks prior. On release day, if metrics are merely expected, smart money unloads, producing a deep retracement.
- **Earnings Surprises:** High surprise gaps price past 50/200 SMAs. Follow-through typically lasts 3 to 5 trading days.
