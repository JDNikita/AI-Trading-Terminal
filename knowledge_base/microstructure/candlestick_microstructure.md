# Advanced Candlestick & Order Book Microstructure Manual

## 1. Bullish & Bearish Engulfing: Institutional Mechanics
- **Order Flow Reality:** An engulfing candle is not merely a visual pattern; it represents a violent liquidity sweep where institutional block orders absorb all resting limit orders on one side of the book.
- **The Bullish Engulfing Signature:** 
  1. The prior candle closes near its low on declining volume, trapping late breakout shorts.
  2. The engulfing session opens at or below the previous close, triggering stop-losses for those shorts.
  3. Heavy institutional accumulation steps in, driving the price past the open of the prior candle.
- **Invalidation & Fakeout Rules:** If the volume on the engulfing candle is lower than the average of the last 10 periods, the pattern is classified as a low-conviction retail trap and must be ignored.

## 2. Rejection Wicks & Liquidity Pools (Pinbars / Hammers / Shooting Stars)
- **The Anatomy of a Trap:** Wicks that extend 2.5x to 3x the real body represent stop-hunts. Market makers push price past obvious technical resistance or support to fill massive institutional size orders, then immediately reverse direction.
- **Location Requirements:** A hammer or shooting star formed in the middle of a consolidation range has a ~42% win rate. The exact same pattern formed at a multi-month Volume Profile Point of Control (POC) or high-volume node jumps to a ~78% win rate.
