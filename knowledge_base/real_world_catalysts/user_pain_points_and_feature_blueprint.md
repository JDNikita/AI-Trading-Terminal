# Retail User Pain-Point Intelligence & Copilot Feature Blueprint


**Research Report: Retail Trader Complaints and AI Trading Software Analysis**

**Introduction**

As a senior fintech product architect and lead quant engineer, I conducted an exhaustive analysis of retail trader complaints from popular online forums (r/algotrading, r/Daytrading, r/wallstreetbets) and existing commercial AI trading software (TrendSpider, Trade Ideas, Danelfin). This report highlights the top 5 fatal flaws of current AI trading software and identifies the key features that retail traders actually want.

**Fatal Flaws of Current AI Trading Software**

### 1. **Black-box signals with no explanations**

* Retail traders are frustrated with AI trading software that provides cryptic signals without providing any clear explanations or context.
* This lack of transparency makes it difficult for traders to understand the reasoning behind the signal and make informed decisions.
* **Recommendation:** Provide clear and concise explanations for each signal, including the underlying data and algorithms used.

### 2. **Inability to factor in breaking news**

* Retail traders are often caught off guard by unexpected market events, such as economic announcements or company earnings reports.
* AI trading software should be able to incorporate real-time news sentiment and market data to provide more accurate signals.
* **Recommendation:** Integrate real-time news sentiment analysis and market data into the AI trading software to provide more accurate and timely signals.

### 3. **Clunky subscription paywalls**

* Retail traders are frustrated with AI trading software that requires a subscription or has restrictive free versions.
* This can create a barrier to entry for new traders and limit the software's potential.
* **Recommendation:** Offer flexible pricing options, including a free version or a subscription model with clear pricing and no hidden fees.

### 4. **Repainting indicators**

* Retail traders are annoyed with AI trading software that uses repainting indicators, which can create false signals and lead to over-trading.
* Repainting indicators can also create a false sense of security, leading traders to over-leverage their accounts.
* **Recommendation:** Use non-repainting indicators that provide accurate and reliable signals.

### 5. **False alerts during chop**

* Retail traders are frustrated with AI trading software that provides false alerts during periods of market consolidation or chop.
* This can lead to over-trading and increased risk of losses.
* **Recommendation:** Implement a robust alert system that filters out false signals during periods of market consolidation.

**What Retail Traders ACTUALLY Want**

### 1. **Real-time news sentiment integration**

* Retail traders want AI trading software that can incorporate real-time news sentiment analysis to provide more accurate signals.
* This can help traders stay ahead of the market and make more informed decisions.

### 2. **Plain-English trade reasoning**

* Retail traders want clear and concise explanations for each trade suggestion, including the underlying data and algorithms used.
* This can help traders understand the reasoning behind the signal and make informed decisions.

### 3. **Dynamic risk-to-reward position sizing**

* Retail traders want AI trading software that can dynamically adjust position sizing based on market conditions and risk tolerance.
* This can help traders manage risk and maximize returns.

### 4. **Live short-term tactical forecasts**

* Retail traders want AI trading software that can provide live short-term tactical forecasts to help them stay ahead of the market.
* This can include forecasts for specific stocks, indices, or market sectors.

### 5. **User-friendly interface and mobile optimization**

* Retail traders want AI trading software with a user-friendly interface and mobile optimization to allow them to trade on-the-go.
* This can include features such as push notifications, mobile trading apps, and easy account management.

**Conclusion**

The analysis of retail trader complaints and existing commercial AI trading software reveals several fatal flaws that can be addressed to improve the user experience. By incorporating real-time news sentiment integration, plain-English trade reasoning, dynamic risk-to-reward position sizing, live short-term tactical forecasts, and user-friendly interfaces, AI trading software can provide a more comprehensive and user-friendly experience for retail traders.
